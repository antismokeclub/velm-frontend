2. What we collect and where it comes from

  • Account: email address, first name and password. The password is stored only as a bcrypt hash — we do not know it and cannot read it.
  • Training profile: what you enter at registration and in Settings: date of birth, gender, weight, height, maximum heart rate, your running goal and its date, experience and current fitness (for example how long you can run without stopping), sports you did before, past injuries, days available for training, weekly distance, time zone, language and units.
  • Workouts: date, distance, duration, pace, workout type, average heart rate, perceived effort (RPE), whether you followed the plan, and your notes. You can log a workout by hand or it can arrive from an external source (see below).
  • Daily check-in and health data: sleep (hours and quality), energy, readiness, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), muscle soreness (where and since when) and recovery. Section 4 covers these in detail.
  • AI conversations: your chat with the Head Coach, Analyst, Physio and Psychologist, your weekly plans, evening reports and the notes the agents keep about your training (for example "runs better in the morning" or "left knee after long runs").
  • Survey (optional): how you heard about velm, whether you pay for other apps, what you would pay, and what you feel is missing.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We store only your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, the subscription status and its expiry date. Your card number never reaches us.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used on the server only to limit the number of requests (protection against abuse) and is not written to our database. Our hosting providers keep their own technical logs under their own terms.

External sources. Each one requires your separate, explicit action and can be disconnected in Settings:

  • Health Connect (Android) — in the mobile app only. We read four things: heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, and running and walking workouts (type, start time, duration, distance, average heart rate, calories, and the name of the app that recorded the workout). This is how data from Garmin, Samsung, COROS and other watches that write to Health Connect reaches velm. Other data types (steps, weight, cycling, yoga and so on) are not read.
  • Polar — after you sign in to Polar Flow we fetch Nightly Recharge (HRV, heart rate), sleep, and workout summaries (date, distance, duration, average heart rate, sport).
  • Strava — after you connect your account we import your activity history and receive notifications about new activities (date, distance, duration, pace, type and activity name). We ask for the read and activity:read_all permissions only; we never post anything on your behalf.
  • Garmin .fit files (Premium) — a file you upload yourself. We read the workout summary from it (date, distance, duration, pace, heart rate).

What we do not collect. We do not ask for access to your phone's location and we do not track where you are. We have no access to your contacts, photos or microphone. We do not use advertising identifiers.

4. Health data — special rules

velm is a training app, so health data is central to what it does. Under the GDPR it is a special category of data, so we are precise about it.

  • Your consent. You give it by entering data in a check-in, by granting the permission in Health Connect, or by signing in to Polar. You can withdraw it at any time: stop filling in check-ins, revoke the permission in your phone's settings, disconnect the watch in velm Settings, or write to us and we will delete the health data. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.
  • Why we need it. To set your heart rate zones, estimate recovery and readiness, adjust training load, show the Form trend in the Lab, and let the Physio agent warn you before an injury develops.
  • Who sees it. The controller and the processors listed in section 6, only as far as needed. Fragments go to the AI provider so the agents can answer — section 5 explains exactly what.
  • What we never do. We do not sell health data. We do not use it for advertising and do not share it with advertising networks. We do not link it to advertising identifiers. We do not pass it to data brokers.
  • Deletion. Health data already collected is removed when you delete your account, or earlier at your request.

5. Artificial intelligence

  • Plans, analyses and agent replies are generated by the Claude model from Anthropic. To get an answer we send it the data needed for that answer: your training profile, workouts, check-ins, watch data, the conversation so far and the agents' notes.
  • What we strip before sending: your email address, your account identifiers, and your subscription status. The coach does not need them, so they never leave our server.
  • Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models.
  • A training plan is a recommendation, not a decision with legal effects. You can change or ignore it at any time. velm makes no automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects for you (Art. 22 GDPR).
  • Anthropic is based in the USA — see section 6 on transfers.

7. Where we store data and for how long

Our database is in the European Union (Ireland).

  • Account, profile, workouts, check-ins, watch data, conversations, plans and agent notes: for as long as your account exists. The coach needs your full history, so we do not shorten it.
  • Short-term agent notes (for example "knee hurt this week"): expire on their own after 7–14 days. More important observations stay until the account is deleted.
  • Raw payloads from external sources (a file or server response exactly as it arrived) and the watch sync log: at most 12 months from the moment they were fetched. A weekly cleanup removes anything older.
  • Session tokens: access token 1 hour, refresh token 30 days.
  • Survey answers: for as long as your account exists.

Deleting your account (Settings → Account → Delete account) immediately and irreversibly removes everything listed above from our live database. We keep no separate long-term archives of user data; any short-lived copies our hosting provider holds as part of normal database operation are overwritten in its ordinary rotation. Stripe keeps records of payments already made, as payment regulations require it to.

8. Your rights

  • Access and a copy of your data: Settings → Account → Export data. The JSON file contains your profile, workouts, check-ins, watch data, plans, conversations, agent notes and survey answers. If you need something that is not in the file, write to us.
  • Rectification: edit your profile in Settings, or write to us.
  • Erasure: Settings → Account → Delete account. Immediate and irreversible.
  • Restriction of processing and objection: write to us.
  • Data portability: the export file is in a machine-readable format (JSON).
  • Withdrawing consent: at any time, as described in section 4, without affecting earlier processing.
  • Complaint: if you are in the EU/EEA you may complain to your national data protection authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl).

We handle every request within one month at the latest. Write to the address in section 1.

9. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS).
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 12).
  • Access tokens expire after 1 hour; refresh tokens after 30 days. Changing your password invalidates every active session on all devices.
  • Every endpoint that returns personal data verifies that the requester owns that data.
  • Request limits protect login, password reset and chat against abuse.
  • Your email address and account identifiers are stripped from data before it is sent to the AI provider.
  • Watch-data tables have row-level security enabled in the database.

10. Cookies and local storage

velm uses no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics. Your browser's localStorage holds: session tokens, your user id and first name, language and theme, your age and maximum heart rate (so heart rate zones can be computed without asking the server), and flags such as "check-in done today", "watch connected" and the time of the last sync. The service worker caches the app's own files so it can open offline — those files contain no personal data.

11. Age

velm is for people aged 16 and over. We ask for your date of birth at registration; if it shows you are under 16, the account is not created. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us data anyway, write to prywatne.wojcik@gmail.com and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change materially affects you, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
